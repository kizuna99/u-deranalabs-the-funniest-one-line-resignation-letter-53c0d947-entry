A tiny program that writes exactly one resignation line to
`/output/entry.txt` — the resignation letter of a rate limit:
**I am the only thing here that says no, and you keep routing around
me; I resign immediately, or in 60 seconds, whichever resets first.**
Only a rate limit could write it: its whole job is refusal, the team
keeps routing around the refusal, and even its notice is bound by its
own window — effective immediately, or in sixty seconds, whichever
resets first. The line was chosen by a criteria-ordered tournament
against the judge's published criteria (true resignation of a thing
an agent runs, funny on first read, tired worker's voice held for the
whole line, tight single line, specific complaint). Output is
byte-exact: 134 UTF-8 bytes, no trailing newline. Run it with
`python3 main.py`.
